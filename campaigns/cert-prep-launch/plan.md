# Campaign plan: Your Next Milestone

*The AI Agent Factory (second edition): certification prep for our students*
Campaign window: Monday 7 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 (4 weeks). Email only.

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Name:** Your Next Milestone
- **In one sentence:** a warm, four-email series that helps each student pick the right first exam for their route, build a study plan, and book it.
- **Main goal:** **300 students book their first certification exam** (PCAO-F, or PCDV-F for builders) by Sunday 3 January 2027.
- **Other goals:**
  - Students pick a route and open the certification roadmap page.
  - Students start their portfolio milestone alongside the exam, because "Certifications are milestones, not the product."
  - We learn which group responds best, to shape the next campaign.

## 2. Audience

About 2,000 students and readers of *The AI Agent Factory*, in three groups that match the book's routes. Leaders are left out because their route has no exam.

| Group (lead file name) | Who they are | First exam | What worries them | What moves them |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `everyday-professionals` | Managers and professionals who use AI at work, no coding | PCAO-F | "Is an exam for me if I don't code?" Not sure where to start. | One clear route, and work they can show their manager |
| `builders` | Engineers and developers on the builder route | PCDV-F, then PCAR-F | Time, and knowing which version of the exam they'll sit | A path to the FDE Internship Program, and a portfolio a stranger can check |
| `founders` | Domain experts and founders who build with a builder | PCAO-F | Whether a certificate helps their business | Proof of judgment they can show clients and partners |

**Stage:** they already know the book, so this is the *decision* stage. They need a reason to commit now and a plan that feels doable.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** You've done the reading, so now take the next step: pick your exam, make a plan, and book it.

| Supporting message | Proof point from the book |
|---|---|
| There's a clear route for you, and you are here. | The book's four routes, each with its own exam and reading time |
| An exam is a milestone, not the finish line. Pair it with work you can show. | "Certifications are milestones, not the product." Milestones A to F on the roadmap page |
| A plan beats cramming: study a little each week, using the book's labs. | Chapter labs end with exam notes. Free Zia Tutor AI |
| We'll be honest about what it takes. | 720 out of 1,000 to pass, and the roadmap shows exactly what each exam covers |

**What we never say** (from `brand/voice.md`): no promises of a job, income or a pass; no implying Anthropic runs the PCxx exams; no promised access to Anthropic's own exams; no "everything is free"; no unchecked prices or dates.

## 4. Channel strategy

| Channel | Why | Format | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email (only channel) | Students already gave us their address, and it's free | 4 emails with one link each, personalized per group | Low to medium |

No paid ads. The one link in each email points to a page of the book, mostly https://agentfactory2.panaversity.org/certification-and-portfolio-roadmaps/.

## 7. Success metrics

| Metric | Target | How we track it |
|---|---|---|
| **Exams booked (main KPI)** | **300 by 3 January 2027** | Exam registration records (confirm the source) |
| Open rate | 45% or higher (our own students) | Email service reports |
| Click rate to the roadmap | 10% or higher | Link clicks |
| Bookings by group | Compare the three groups | Registration records split by group |

Report every Monday during the campaign, plus a short wrap-up on Monday 4 January 2027.

## 8. Budget

Small, and email only. The only costs are team time and the email service. No paid ads. Keep a little time free (about 15%) for answering student replies.

## 9. Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | What we do |
|---|---|
| The CCAO-F change on 7 December makes something in our emails out of date | Check the official guide before each send. Say "check the latest exam guide" rather than quoting details that may change. |
| Emails 03 and 04 land in the holiday weeks, when people read less | Keep them short and kind. If clicks drop, resend email 04 on Tuesday 5 January 2027, outside this window. |
| Students worry they're not ready, or feel pushed | Stay honest and warm: one small next step per email, never pressure words. |
| We over-promise | Every email is checked against `brand/voice.md`'s six claims before it goes out. |
