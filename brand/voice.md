# Brand voice: The AI Agent Factory (second edition)

**Who we are:** Panaversity, publisher of *The AI Agent Factory*, second edition. It's a free book by Zia Khan and four co-authors, at https://agentfactory2.panaversity.org/.
**Who we write to:** our students and readers.
**What our emails do:** help students plan how they prepare for the certifications (PCAO-F, PCDV-F, PCAR-F, PCAR-P) and build a portfolio, following https://agentfactory2.panaversity.org/certification-and-portfolio-roadmaps/.

## 1. Our voice in three words

- **Warm:** we write like a teacher who's glad you showed up. Speak to "you", one student at a time.
- **Encouraging:** every email leaves the reader with one clear next step they can take today. Celebrate progress, however small.
- **Honest:** say what the exams and milestones really ask of you. Never oversell, and never hide the hard parts.

Write short, plain sentences. Explain any jargon the first time it appears.

## 2. Words we use

Use the book's own words, so the emails and the book feel like one voice:

- AI Worker, managed (not prompted)
- milestone, as in "Certifications are milestones, not the product."
- a portfolio a stranger can check
- you are here
- Four-Part Brief, Review Contract, Authority Envelope
- route, as in your route through the book (everyday professional, builder, domain expert or founder, leader)
- exam codes written exactly: PCAO-F, PCDV-F, PCAR-F, PCAR-P, plus the FDE Internship Program
- next step, practice, build, show your work

## 3. Words we avoid

Hype and pressure words. They break our "honest" promise:

- guaranteed, guarantee
- hack, shortcut, crush it, 10x
- easy, effortless
- last chance, limited seats, hurry, act now, don't miss out

## 4. Claims we must never make

1. **No job or income guarantee.** No certification on its own guarantees a job or income, and the book says so itself.
2. **No "pass guaranteed".** Never promise a pass, a score or a timeline, like "pass PCAO-F in a weekend".
3. **Don't imply Anthropic endorses or runs the PCxx exams.** The PCxx exams are Panaversity's own. Panaversity is a member of Anthropic's Claude Partner Network, and we can say that, but no more.
4. **Don't promise access to Anthropic's own exams** (CCAO-F, CCAR-F and the others). They need a work email from a Claude Partner Network organization, or a place in the FDE Internship Program.
5. **Don't say "everything is free".** The book and Zia Tutor AI are free. Some later exercises need a paid Claude or ChatGPT plan, and builders pay for API usage.
6. **No exam prices or dates unless they've been checked** against the official page first. Exam versions change, for example CCAO-F updates on December 7, 2026.

## 5. How we sign off

End every email with:

> You've got this, and see you at the next milestone.
> Zia Khan and the Panaversity team
